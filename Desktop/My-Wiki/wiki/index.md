## Book — The Denial of Death (Becker, 1973)

- [[book-denial-of-death/overview]] — Central thesis: death terror as the primary engine of civilization; man as "gods with anuses"; codified hero systems; key thinkers (source: The Denial of Death – Becker, 2026-04-09)
- [[book-denial-of-death/terror-of-death]] — Existential paradox, primary repression, the Jonah Syndrome, the body problem, heroism as response to death awareness (source: The Denial of Death – Becker, 2026-04-09)
- [[book-denial-of-death/vital-lie]] — Character as neurotic shield; Perls' four-layer structure; the costs and necessity of the vital lie; why full humanness means full terror (source: The Denial of Death – Becker, 2026-04-09)
- [[book-denial-of-death/kierkegaard-and-rank]] — Kierkegaard's characterology, shut-upness, despair typology (schizophrenia/depression continuum), Otto Rank's synthesis (source: The Denial of Death – Becker, 2026-04-09)
- [[book-denial-of-death/heroic-individual]] — Limits of psychotherapy, Freud vs. Kierkegaard, utopians of unrepression (Brown/Marcuse), Tillich's courage to be, Rank's final proposal (source: The Denial of Death – Becker, 2026-04-09)

## Book — Escape From Evil (Becker, 1975)

- [[book-escape-from-evil/overview]] — Central thesis: man's ingenuity (not animal nature) is the root of evil; mortality terror drives cultural heroism which generates scapegoating and war (source: Escape From Evil – Becker, 2026-04-07)
- [[book-escape-from-evil/immortality-projects]] — Ritual → kingship → money as successive immortality ideologies; the *causa sui* project; why economic equality is "beyond endurance" (source: Escape From Evil – Becker, 2026-04-07)
- [[book-escape-from-evil/evil-and-scapegoating]] — The basic dynamic: killing others buys off one's own death; sacrifice, war, victimage, the survivor dynamic, Burke's sociological formula (source: Escape From Evil – Becker, 2026-04-07)
- [[book-escape-from-evil/heroic-society]] — Social theory conclusion: merger of Marx and Freud; limits of Enlightenment; what a less destructive heroism would require (source: Escape From Evil – Becker, 2026-04-07)

- [[book-western-attitudes-toward-death/thy-death]] — Ch. III: Romantic shift; death as rupture; cult of the other's death; cemetery cult; will secularization (source: Ariés_Philippe_Western_Attitudes_Toward_Death_1976.pdf, 2026-04-09)

## 랑크–베커–공포관리이론 계보

- [[rank-becker-tmt/overview]] — Intellectual genealogy connecting Kierkegaard → Rank → Becker → TMT; why death-awareness is the hidden engine of civilization and how that claim moved from philosophy to empirical science (source: raw/랑크_베커_공포관리이론.md, 2026-06-13)
- [[rank-becker-tmt/death-immortality-history]] — Neanderthal burial (120,000 BP) through Mesopotamia, Egypt, Greece, Christianity, Enlightenment, and 20th-century secularization; establishing that the death-denial drive is a species-wide constant, not a modern pathology (source: raw/랑크_베커_공포관리이론.md, 2026-06-13)
- [[rank-becker-tmt/kierkegaard]] — Kierkegaard's life (1813–1855); self as finite/infinite synthesis; anxiety (*Angst*) as the dizziness of freedom; three forms of despair; three stages; knight of faith; documented influence on Rank and Becker (source: raw/랑크_베커_공포관리이론.md, 2026-06-13)
- [[rank-becker-tmt/tmt]] — TMT origins (Skidmore 1980, hostile SESP reception, decade-long rejection); core theory (cultural worldviews + self-esteem as anxiety buffers); dual-process model; key experiments (Arizona judges ×9, worldview defense, Bush/MS politics, consumerism, afterlife belief elimination of MS effects); Many Labs 4 replication failure; *Worm at the Core* (source: raw/랑크_베커_공포관리이론.md, 2026-06-13)
- [[rank-becker-tmt/self-esteem-ms]] — Harmon-Jones et al. (1997) JPSP: 3 experiments showing that both manipulated and dispositional high self-esteem eliminate mortality salience worldview-defense effects; Experiment 3 identifies the mechanism — self-esteem maintains suppression of death constructs across the post-MS delay interval (source: Terror_Management_Theory_and_Self-Esteem.pdf, 2026-06-18)

## Thinkers
